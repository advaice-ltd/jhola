Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Jhola is a shared grocery list app made by Advaice Limited ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we don't collect any of your data. There is no Jhola account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

Jhola stores the things you put into it: your grocery items, amounts, notes, when items were added or bought, and the first name you type in so your partner can see who added what.

  • On your iPhone. The list is saved on your device.
  • In your iCloud account. If you are signed in to iCloud, the list is copied to your private iCloud storage using Apple's CloudKit, so it can sync and be shared. This data is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Advaice Limited cannot see it, and we run no servers of our own.

Sharing your list

When you invite someone to your list, Apple's iCloud sharing sends them an invite link. Everyone on a shared list can see and change its items and see the names attached to them. You can stop sharing, or leave a shared list, at any time from the Household screen in the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you tap the microphone button, Jhola uses Apple's speech recognition to turn what you say into list items. Depending on your device, Apple may process the audio on your iPhone or on its servers. Jhola does not record, keep or send your audio anywhere else. The microphone is only used while the voice panel is open.

Siri

If you use "Hey Siri, add to Jhola", your request is handled by Apple's Siri, and the items are added to your list on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Jhola shows a notice on your phone when your partner adds items. These notices are created on your device. We never send marketing messages.

Links to shops

Jhola can open a search for an item on online grocery services. When you follow one of these links you leave Jhola, and that service's own privacy policy applies. Jhola does not share your list with them.

Deleting your data

  • Remove items in the app by swiping them away.
  • Deleting the app removes the copy on your iPhone.
  • To delete the iCloud copy, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then manage your storage, and delete the data for Jhola.

Children

Jhola is a general household app and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
