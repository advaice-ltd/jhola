Sharing with your partner

How do we share one list?
  1. Both of you install Jhola.
  2. One of you opens the people icon at the top right, then taps Invite your partner.
  3. Send the invite on WhatsApp or Messages.
  4. Your partner taps the link. From then on you both see the same list.
My partner tapped the link but nothing happened

Check that Jhola is installed on their iPhone and that they are signed in to iCloud (Settings, then their name at the top). Then ask them to tap the link again. If they already had items in Jhola, those are kept and added to the shared list.

Changes aren't showing up on the other phone

Both phones need an internet connection and iCloud. Pull down on the list to sync straight away. Changes made offline sync as soon as you're back online.

How do we stop sharing?

Open the people icon, then Manage sharing (or Manage or leave if you joined someone else's list). Whoever leaves keeps a copy of the list on their phone.

Using Jhola

Can I add several things at once?

Yes. Separate them with commas or "and": 2 kg onion, 1 L milk and dhania. You can also paste a list straight from WhatsApp.

Which amounts does it understand?

kg, g, litre, ml, packet, dozen, bunch, piece and more, plus everyday words like half kg, paav kilo (250 g), do kilo and dedh litre.

Can I add items by voice?

Tap the microphone in the add bar and say what you need. You can also say "Hey Siri, add to Jhola".

Where do ticked items go?

They move to "In the jhola" at the bottom of the list. Tap Clear when you're done, or leave them and they tidy themselves away the next day. They stay in your Usuals so you can add them again in one tap.
